## Outcome

Persist ownership before process launch and retain it through continuations.

Traceability: #533:4, #533:2. These are source-plan references, not dependency IDs.

## Required behavior

1. Create or load the exact attempt from admitted content before launching CODING; provide its identity to the wrapper ownership marker and current run record.
2. Continuations and FIX for the same active attempt reuse that context, while changed admitted content cannot silently attach to it.
3. A persistence/identity conflict stops before launch; no launch should exist without reconstructable ownership.

## Acceptance criteria

- Failure before durable binding launches no coder.
- Retry/continuation retains the same attempt; another admitted fingerprint creates another identity.
- The launch callback/current run and process marker agree on attempt identity.
