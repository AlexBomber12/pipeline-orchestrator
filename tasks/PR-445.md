## Outcome

Make a Git-edited task obey the same rejection/readmission rules as an uploaded one.

Traceability: #533:20. These are source-plan references, not dependency IDs.

## Required behavior

1. Snapshot relevant local task content/ownership before repository synchronization can overwrite it; compare candidate content against the durable rejected fingerprint afterward.
2. Reserve/admit only the newly eligible exact bytes through the common service and carry that binding to execution.
3. Do not replay cached pre-Reject content, restore the old spec over the accepted one, or bypass a pending/unknown fence through Git edits.

## Acceptance criteria

- Changed content committed after rejection can become one new attempt.
- Unchanged or stale cached content remains fenced across fetch/reset/restart.
- Git-edit and upload paths make equivalent decisions for identical history and bytes.
