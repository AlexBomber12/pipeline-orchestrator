## Scope

1. error: the pipeline cannot proceed without operator action, or a step failed (status write failed, push failed, admission failed).
2. warn: degraded or deferred with a known retry, when it delays work.
3. ok: a step completed (pushed, merged, applied, recovered, cleared after Retry).
4. info: normal progress or waiting (waiting for CI, picked task, starting).
5. "Picked task ..." -> info; "[BACKUP] bundle creation crashed" -> error; "No tasks available." -> info.
